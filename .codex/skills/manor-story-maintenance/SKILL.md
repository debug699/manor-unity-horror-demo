# Manor Story Maintenance

Read the background guide first and then `05_剧情圣经_StoryBible.md`. Add `02_游戏设计文档_GDD.md` when the change affects playable flow, puzzles, UI, or enemy rules. Do not read unrelated art or technical documents unless the task needs them.

## Canon lock

- Modern setting; fictional 法圣 only.
- Four offerings: pregnant adult woman, yin-born boy, yin-born girl, and qualifying adult man.
- The children come from different families, are not twins, and are not the pregnant woman's children.
- Tom is a modern private investigator with no supernatural powers or weapons.
- The pregnant wraith is a victim whose hostility comes from pain and misunderstanding.
- The butcher is a living active perpetrator, about 80 years old and sustained to look about 50.
- No combat, weapons, multiplayer, progression, branches, or multiple endings.

## Workflow

1. Identify affected timeline, character knowledge, clue order, objective, and ending state.
2. Check every new fact against canon and report conflicts before writing.
3. Prefer environmental evidence and short fragments over exposition dumps.
4. Keep the first Demo linear: exploration and clues, parchment communication, ritual collapse, eight-minute chase, dawn, bridge, password, escape.
5. Update the smallest appropriate document; do not silently rewrite other project bibles.
6. Provide an impact note and acceptance checks for any story change.
